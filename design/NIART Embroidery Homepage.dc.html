Custom orders

How a piece comes together

  1. 01

    Consult

    An hour with a designer, in studio or on video. Bring references, photographs, an old saree.

  2. 02

    Sketch & Fabric

    A hand drawing and three fabric options, couriered to you. Two revisions included.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Fourteen measurements, taken in person or with our guide. One fitting at the muslin stage.

  4. 04

    Hand-embroider

    Four to six weeks on the frame. Photographs at every stage, and a final fitting before dispatch.

The atelier

Above a fabric street in Coimbatore

Twenty karigars work here. Most were trained by the two who started with us in 2008. A bridal lehenga takes between 300 and 600 hours on the adda frame, and we do not shorten that.

Zardozi, aari and kamdani were learned in workshops in Lucknow and Bhuj. We teach them the same way here — at the frame, over years.
