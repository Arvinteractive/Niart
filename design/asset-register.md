# Launch asset register

The 13 WebP images imported by `src/assets/images.js` and the social image
`public/og-image.jpg` derive from generated design imagery. See
`design/image-prompts.md` for the available prompt history. They are publicly
labelled AI-generated illustrations, not completed NIART commissions or stock.
No original studio photography has been supplied for this launch update.

The site does not display the mockup prices, hours of production, founding year,
artisan count or exact studio coordinates. Instagram is the confirmed contact
channel; email, WhatsApp and a street address require owner confirmation.

Before replacing the illustrations, record the photographer or creator, source,
rights holder, written commercial-use permission and any applicable model/property
releases for each supplied image. AI disclosure is not evidence of rights clearance.
The owner must confirm the generated assets' commercial-use rights and approval
of the NIART mark; this repository does not contain those records.

React, React DOM and their bundled dependencies' notices are generated at build
time as `/third-party-licenses.md`. The three Google Fonts are Cormorant Garamond,
Inter and Marcellus, served by Google Fonts. Their licence notices are recorded
in `/font-licenses.md`.

The privacy page describes the current static site and its external services.
The studio's actual handling of enquiries, retention and applicable legal duties
still needs owner/legal review; code checks do not verify those business practices.
