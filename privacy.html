Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

NIART (“NIART”, “we”, “us”) publishes niart.in to introduce our embroidery services and illustrative design ideas. This page explains what information the site gathers, why, and what choices you have. It is written to match what the site actually does today — not a generic template — so please read it as a description of the current site rather than a promise about features we haven’t built.

Information we collect

Browsing niart.in itself does not require you to create an account or submit any personal information. If you choose to contact us — for example by messaging our Instagram profile about a piece — we receive whatever you include in that message: typically your name, contact details, and the contents of your enquiry. We use that information only to respond to you.

Cookies and tracking

niart.in does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking technologies of any kind. If this changes, this page will be updated to describe the new technology and applicable choices.

Third-party services

The site loads three typefaces from Google Fonts at fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com. Loading a font from Google’s servers means your browser makes a direct request to Google, which can see basic technical information about that request (such as your IP address) in the same way any web request reveals it to the server handling it. Google’s handling of that data is governed by Google’s own privacy policy. Vercel, our hosting provider, may also keep standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability purposes, for operating the website. Contact links open Instagram (Meta); its privacy policy applies when you visit or send a message there. No Instagram content is embedded on this website.

How we use information

We use the contents of an enquiry solely to answer it — for example, to discuss a piece, timelines, or pricing. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data retention

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the conversation and any resulting commission, and to meet our own record-keeping obligations. You can contact us to discuss a request to remove correspondence. Copies held by the messaging service are also subject to its own retention rules.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, request a copy of it, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, through the contact profile below. We will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law (for visitors in India, this includes rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where applicable).

Children’s privacy

This site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If what the site does changes — for instance, if we ever add analytics, cookies, or online checkout — we will update this page and the “last updated” date above accordingly.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a request relating to your information, can be sent to the studio’s Instagram profile.
